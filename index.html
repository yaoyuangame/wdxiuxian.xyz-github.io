Forced Waits
一 · 工坊

Fewer systems, held longer.

We build games that ask for a minute, not an evening. Each one is a single clear idea, refined until nothing extra remains — and then supported for years instead of abandoned at the next release window.

静

Quiet by design

No timers that punish you for leaving, no streaks that break, no countdown offers. Nothing in our games decays while you live your life.

简

One idea per title

A game should be explainable in a sentence. If the pitch needs a diagram, the design is not finished yet.

续

Kept, not shelved

Titles from 2020 still receive compatibility work. A release is the beginning of upkeep, never the end of it.

二 · 作品

The flagship, and what it refuses to do.

旗 舰

WeiXiao — a puzzle with no clock.

Our flagship release is a hand-authored deduction puzzle. There is no timer, no fail state, and no resource that drains while you think about the next move.

Each board introduces a single idea, then asks you to combine it with everything the game has already taught. Hints are free and unlimited, progress is written on every move, and the whole game runs offline.

三 · 律

Four rules we do not break.

壹

No manufactured waiting

Energy bars and cooldowns are not added to games that do not need them. Difficulty belongs in the puzzle, never in the shop.

贰

Price before the tap

Anything purchasable is priced and described before you commit. No invented scarcity, no recurring “limited” offers.

叁

Minimal collection

We gather only what is required to run and improve the game, stated plainly in our privacy policy.

肆

Fix before feature

A known crash is repaired before new content begins. A broken build is not a roadmap item — it is a broken product.
